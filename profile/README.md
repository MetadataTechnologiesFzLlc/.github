# Metadata Technologies FZ LLC

Metadata Technologies FZ LLC develops business software for the real estate sector. Its Salesforce practice develops **PropertyFlex**, a suite of Salesforce solutions for real estate sales and leasing.

## PropertyFlex

PropertyFlex brings customer, property, and unit records together in Salesforce. It helps developers and property teams track each customer’s progress, from the first enquiry through a sale or lease.

### Real Estate Sales

PropertyFlex Sales supports lead capture and qualification, unit search and shortlisting, sales proposals, offers, and reservations. Sales teams can manage payment plans and track post-sale activities such as unit inspections and handover.

### Real Estate Leasing

PropertyFlex Leasing helps leasing teams manage prospective tenants, match their requirements to available units, and track each enquiry through the lease process. Teams can keep tenant details, unit availability, and lease records connected in Salesforce.
